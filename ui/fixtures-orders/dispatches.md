# Dispatches

## Sol 1
Both rovers left base for objective 1, the search zone D5-F7. rover-1 reached C3
and scanned. rover-2 drove into rough ground at D4, stopped there broken, and left
a note for the field engineer. Oxygen fell to eleven of twelve sols.

## Sol 2
rover-1 crossed into the zone at D5 and found colonist one on its own tile:
aboard, objective one closed, the clock stopped. rover-2 stayed broken at D4 and
the engineer's repair was held over. The engine handed control back to the officer
on duty: continue, rest, or abort.

## Sol 3
Orders received: rest and repair. No clock was running, so the sol cost the
colonists nothing; both rovers came back online where they stood. Two of three
colonists are still out there and the team is waiting for orders again.
